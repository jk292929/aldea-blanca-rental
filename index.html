The house

A private retreat, minutes from Puerto Banús

Set in Aldea Blanca, one of the most sought-after urbanisations in Nueva Andalucía, this home offers something remarkably rare for the area: its own private swimming pool, which can be heated on request so it's a pleasure to enjoy all year round. Guests also have access to three further communal pools within the urbanisation.

Combine that with a large 585 m² plot and calm, minimalist Scandinavian interiors, and you have a private retreat just minutes from Puerto Banús and Marbella's finest beaches.

Step inside and you are greeted by a spacious, open-plan living and dining area, full of natural light and furnished with clean lines, muted tones and natural materials. The space flows into a fully equipped kitchen with a sleek island and high-end appliances — everything you need to cook properly rather than just get by.

Across 180 m² of interior space, the house sleeps up to seven guests in four bedrooms — three doubles and one single — and three modern shower bathrooms, with luxurious Scandinavian finishes throughout.

The real heart of this home, though, is outdoors. The private garden is an unusually generous space for a townhouse in this part of Marbella, with mature trees for shade, landscaped planting, sun loungers and a hammock for soaking up the Andalusian sun.

A fully equipped BBQ area and an outdoor dining table make long al fresco meals effortless, and a cosy lounge corner is the perfect spot for morning coffee or an evening glass of wine — all in complete privacy, right beside your own pool.
