Questions

Before you book

Is the pool private?

Yes. It belongs to the house and is not shared — this is the only home in Aldea Blanca with its own heated pool. The urbanisation's three communal pools, one of them very large, are open to guests as well.

Can the pool be heated?

Yes, on request, for the nightly supplement shown in the rates above. Ask for it when you book so it is warm when you arrive.

How many guests can stay?

Seven, in four bedrooms — three doubles and one single — with three shower bathrooms.

Is Puerto Banús walkable?

Yes. The house is in the town itself, and Puerto Banús — the marina, the boutiques and the beach clubs — is within walking distance.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is parking within the urbanisation. Most guests find they use the car far less than they expected.

Who handles the booking?

JustRent Marbella. They hold the availability calendar, quote the price, take the payment and handle guest service.

Where do I see current prices and dates?

On the JustRent Marbella listing. The range is above; the exact price for your dates, and whether they are free, is shown there.

Are parties or pets allowed?

No. The house takes families and couples, with no groups under 30 and no hen or stag parties. It is non-smoking and pets are not accepted.
